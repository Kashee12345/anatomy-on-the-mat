How every lesson is built

See it, feel it, cue it, check it

See itShort anatomy segment with labelled diagrams, one structure at a time.
Feel itThe same idea found in a pose, in the trainee's own body.
Cue itTranslated into plain words a new teacher can say in class.
Check itThree quick questions and a one-page summary to keep.
Your instructor

Dr. Akash Garg, D.C.

I am a chiropractor and biology instructor who has taught anatomy and physiology since 2007, mostly to students with no medical background. I teach Human Anatomy & Physiology full time at the University of South Carolina Upstate, and before that spent eighteen years at Spartanburg Community College, six of them as Science Department Chair.

Chiropractic training shapes how I teach movement: spinal and joint biomechanics, palpation, how load travels through the body, and knowing when a complaint needs a referral rather than a modification. I build interactive study tools like the ones on this site for my own courses, because students remember what they can move and test.
